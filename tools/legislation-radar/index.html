Guide

How Legislation Radar works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

New legislation from the official publishers in one clean feed: EU regulations, directives and decisions from EUR-Lex, new UK legislation (Acts and statutory instruments of the UK, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland) from legislation.gov.uk, US public laws and bill texts from GovInfo, new Australian Acts and legislative instruments from the Federal Register of Legislation, and new Canadian regulations from the Canada Gazette. Filter by keyword, sector and kind; get only what's new; let AI tell you what each law changes and who it affects.

Built for compliance and legal teams, public-affairs consultants, law firms, trade associations, regulatory-intelligence products, and AI agents.

Why this Actor

  • One schema. country, subdivision (for Scottish, Welsh, NI law), kind, number, title, date, stage (bills), sectors, url, pdfUrl.
  • Sector and keyword filters across all five jurisdictions.
  • Law alerts. Turn on Only new laws and schedule it; send results to Slack, Google Sheets, a webhook or an RSS reader.
  • AI insights (optional). What the law does, who is affected, and with your profile a relevance score.
  • For AI agents. Callable as an MCP tool.

Pricing

$3 per 1,000 laws plus a small start fee per run. AI insights, when you turn them on, are $0.01 each, plus the model's tokens: about $0.0003 per insight with the default model (billed to your Apify account by Apify's AI gateway), or your provider's price with your own key.

Good to know

Titles and numbers are as published; the url is the official text. Gov Radar covers the news, consultations and guidance around new law; Legislation Radar covers the law itself.

Questions

Questions about Legislation Radar

How much does Legislation Radar cost?

$3 per 1,000, charged only for law or bills returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Legislation Radar return?

Every row has the same fields, including date, country, kind, number, title, stage, sectors, url. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as search_legislation.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
