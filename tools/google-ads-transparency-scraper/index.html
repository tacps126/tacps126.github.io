Guide

How Google Ads Transparency Center Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Get every ad a brand runs on Google from the Google Ads Transparency Center: Search, YouTube, Display, Maps, Play and Shopping. Paste a website or brand name and get the ad text, headline, images, videos, call to action, landing page, first and last date shown, format, surfaces and countries. No Google account needed.

At a glance

  • Find advertisers your way: website domain (nike.com), brand name as Google verifies it, Google advertiser ID (AR...) or a link copied from the Transparency Center. A domain also finds resellers and partners advertising the brand.
  • Ad creative: headline, description, call to action, display URL, landing page, image and video links, YouTube video, product of shopping ads, and a preview image of every ad.
  • Timing: first and last date shown, running now or stopped, days running. Long-running ads stand out.
  • Video transcripts (optional): the spoken words of each video ad, from its YouTube captions, with their language.
  • Countries and reach (optional): every version of the ad and, for ads that ran in the EU, each country with its own dates, times shown and surfaces.
  • Filters: country, running now or all, date range, ad type (image, video, text), surfaces.
  • Monitoring: turn on Only new ads and schedule the run to get each new ad once.
  • Price in parts, each below the usual Store price: $0.40 per 1,000 ads, plus $0.40 per 1,000 for ad text and landing page (on by default, charged only when the ad has them) $0.40 per 1,000 for countries and reach (off by default) and $5 per 1,000 video ads with a transcript (off by default). A small start fee per run; lower prices on higher Apify plans.

Questions

Questions about Google Ads Transparency Center Scraper

Why does a text ad have no text?

In some countries, the US among them, Google shows text ads only as a picture of the ad. Those rows keep the picture in previewImageUrl. Image, video and shopping ads come with their headline, landing page and media.

Why did a brand name find nothing?

The name must match the advertiser's verified name. Try the website domain or the advertiser ID from the Transparency Center.

Need Meta, LinkedIn or TikTok ads too?

Competitor Ads (tacps126/competitor-ads) reads all four libraries in one run, in the same row shape.

How much does Google Ads Transparency Center Scraper cost?

$0.40 per 1,000, charged only for google ads returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Google Ads Transparency Center Scraper return?

Every row has the same fields, including source, advertiserName, creator, format, headline, text, callToAction, landingUrl. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
