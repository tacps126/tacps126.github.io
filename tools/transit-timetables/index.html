Transit Journey Planner & Timetables — Bus, Metro, Rail

Public transport for ~2,900 cities and operators from their official timetables: journeys from A to B with changes and walking, next departures at a stop, stops near a point and every route. Bus, metro, rail, tram and ferry feeds merged per city.
