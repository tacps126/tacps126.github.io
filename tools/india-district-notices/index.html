Guide

How India District Notices works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Notices from the official websites of India's 748 districts in one feed: district tenders and auctions, recruitment (contract posts, Anganwadi, health and education vacancies) and announcements (public notices, electoral rolls, land acquisition, scheme lists). Pick states or districts and categories; schedule it for daily only-new alerts; let AI summarise Hindi and regional-language notices in English.

Built for local contractors and suppliers, tender aggregators, job portals and coaching institutes, NGOs, journalists and researchers following district administrations, and AI agents.

Coverage

All 748 district websites listed in the National Portal of India's web directory, across every state and union territory. Most use the government's standard district-site template, so each category's current notices and recent archive are read directly; Rajasthan's 40 districts are read from the state portal's own notices API (latest tenders with their last date, orders and press releases) when it accepts the connection: it often refuses connections from outside Indian home networks, and the run then says so; districts on other systems are read from the update lists their home page links to.

Why this Actor

  • District level, everywhere. State and central portals miss most small tenders, auctions and contract jobs that collectors' offices post only on the district site.
  • One schema. district, state, subdivision (ISO, e.g. IN-BR), category, title, date, url, documents (the PDFs), listing (current or archive).
  • Choose easily. States from a searchable list (or type Bihar, IN-UP), districts by name (misspellings get a suggestion), up to 120 districts per run.
  • Daily alerts. Turn on Only new notices and schedule it; send results to Slack, Google Sheets, a webhook or an RSS reader.
  • AI insights (optional). A plain-language summary in your language, what the notice is about, the last date, and with your profile a 0-100 fit score.

Pricing

$3 per 1,000 notices plus a small start fee per run. AI insights, when you turn them on, are $0.01 each, plus the model's tokens: about $0.0003 per insight with the default model (billed to your Apify account by Apify's AI gateway), or your provider's price with your own key. Notices that don't match your filters, and (with Only new notices) notices already delivered, are free.

Good to know

Titles are as published, often in Hindi or the state language; the PDF is the official notice. Some district sites are slow or temporarily down; the run reports them and carries on.

Questions

Questions about India District Notices

How much does India District Notices cost?

$3 per 1,000, charged only for district notices returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does India District Notices return?

Every row has the same fields, including date, district, state, category, title, lastDate, listing, url. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as get_india_district_notices.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
