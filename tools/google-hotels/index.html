Guide

How Google Hotels Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Get hotel prices from Google Hotels for any place and your travel dates as a clean dataset: nightly and total price, with and without taxes and fees, the deal versus the usual price ("32% less than usual"), hotel class, rating and review count, check-in and check-out times, the hotel's own website, coordinates and a Google Hotels link. Export to JSON, CSV or Excel, send to Google Sheets, or call it as an API.

Built for hotel revenue managers watching competitor rates, travel startups and agencies comparing prices, analysts tracking hotel pricing by city and date, and anyone planning trips at scale.

Why this scraper

  • Plain-word places. Lisbon, Shibuya, Tokyo or Times Square, New York.
  • Prices for your dates. Set check-in and check-out: nightly rate, total for the stay, and both with taxes and fees.
  • Deals flagged. Google's "less than usual" deal text when a price is below normal.
  • About 100 hotels per place. More hotels per place also searches each hotel class and vacation rentals, then removes duplicates (they are free).
  • Filters. Minimum hotel class and maximum nightly price; filtered hotels are not charged.
  • Rate tracking. Schedule it daily with the same dates to follow prices over time.

How to use it

  1. Add Locations and Check-in / Check-out dates.
  2. Optionally set filters.
  3. Press Start. Download results from the Output tab, or connect an integration.

{
  "locations": ["Lisbon", "Porto"],

Pricing

Pay per result: about $2 per 1,000 hotels (lower on higher Apify plans) plus a small start fee. Duplicates and filtered hotels are free. See the Pricing tab for exact rates, and set a maximum charge per run to cap spend.

On Apify's free plan each run returns up to 100 hotels. Any paid Apify plan removes the limit.

Questions

Questions about Google Hotels Scraper

Which currency?

Prices are in US dollars.

Can I get each booking site's price for a hotel?

Not yet. You get Google's lowest shown price per hotel. Open an issue if you need the per-site list.

How many hotels per place?

About 100 with More hotels per place on. For more, search neighborhoods (Alfama, Lisbon).

Is it legal?

The Actor collects publicly shown prices. You're responsible for how you use the data under the laws that apply to you.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Google Hotels Scraper return?

Every row has the same fields, including name, hotelClass, stars, price, priceText, deal, rating, reviewsCount, checkInTime, checkOutTime, website, googleUrl, photo, latitude, longitude, countryCode, description. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as search_google_hotels.
