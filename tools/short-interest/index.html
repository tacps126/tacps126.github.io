Guide

How Short Interest & Short Volume works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Short interest for every US stock: NYSE, Nasdaq, NYSE Arca, NYSE American, Cboe and OTC, about 22,000 stocks per settlement date, straight from FINRA's official consolidated short interest. Look up your tickers with up to a year of history, or scan the whole market for the most-shorted stocks with filters. Add the daily short-sale volume and short ratio, or pull the daily files on their own.

Built for traders and short-squeeze screeners, quant and research teams, financial newsletters and dashboards, fintech apps and AI agents.

Why this Actor

  • Whole market in one run. Every listed and OTC stock, not only one exchange: ~22,000 rows in about 10 seconds.
  • Screen it. Minimum days to cover, short interest, change %, average volume and short volume ratio; sort by any of them.
  • History. Up to 24 settlement dates (one year) per ticker.
  • Two datasets. Twice-monthly short interest and daily short volume, joined on request.
  • Alerts. Turn on Only new and schedule it: each new settlement date or trading day arrives once. Send it to Slack, Google Sheets, email or a webhook.
  • AI insights (optional). Squeeze risk, what the numbers say (shorts building or covering) and what to watch next, in plain words; with your profile, a fit score.
  • For AI agents. Callable as an MCP tool.

How to use it

Most-shorted Nasdaq and NYSE stocks on the newest date:

json
{
  "markets": ["Nasdaq", "NYSE"],
  "minDaysToCover": 10,
  "minAvgDailyVolume": 500000,
  "addShortVolume": true,
  "maxItems": 100
}

A year of history for your watchlist:

json
{ "symbols": ["GME", "AMC", "TSLA"], "historyCycles": 24 }

Daily short volume, last 5 trading days, heavy short selling only:

json
{ "dataset": "shortVolume", "shortVolumeDays": 5, "minTotalVolume": 1000000, "minShortRatio": 0.6 }

Pricing

$5 per 1,000 rows (one stock on one date) plus a small start fee per run. AI insights, when you turn them on, are $0.01 each. On the Apify free plan a run returns up to 100 rows.

Good to know

  • FINRA publishes short interest about a week after each settlement date and keeps one rolling year. Daily short volume appears the evening of each trading day.
  • Daily short volume covers trades reported to FINRA's facilities, not every exchange's own trades, so shortRatio is the share of that volume.
  • Figures are as FINRA publishes them. Need another field or market? Contact tacps126@gmail.com.
Questions

Questions about Short Interest & Short Volume

How much does Short Interest & Short Volume cost?

$5 per 1,000, charged only for rows returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Short Interest & Short Volume return?

Every row has the same fields, including symbol, issueName, market, settlementDate, shortInterest, previousShortInterest, change, changePercent, avgDailyVolume, daysToCover, shortVolumeRatio, shortVolumeDays, stockSplit, revised. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
