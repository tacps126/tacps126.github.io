Guide

How Company Registry works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Fresh B2B leads and risk signals straight from official company registers: every new company registered in France (BODACC), Norway (Brønnøysund), Finland (PRH / YTJ), Estonia and the US states New York, Colorado, Connecticut and Oregon, French insolvency judgments, deregistrations and business sales, and company search with legal form, industry, address, employees and officers, plus the worldwide LEI index (GLEIF) for any other country.

Built for B2B sales teams and agencies selling to new businesses (accountants, banks, insurers, payroll, POS, web and telecom), credit-risk and collections teams, KYB and supplier onboarding, market researchers, and AI agents.

At a glance

  • Price: $2.50 per 1,000 companies. Free plan: up to 100 companies per run.
  • First run: the prefilled input returns 40 companies registered this week in Finland, France, Norway and Estonia in about 3 seconds.
  • Sample data: results from a recent run as CSV or JSON. Opens in your browser, no account needed.
  • Free weekly dataset: the last 7 days on Hugging Face, no account needed.

Two modes

  1. New companies and register events (default). Choose countries and a window (registeredWithinDays), filter by industry codes (NACE / NAF, e.g. 56.10 restaurants, 62 IT; NAICS for Connecticut), locations (city, region, US state or county, or postcode prefix) or keywords. For France also choose the events: new companies, insolvencies, deregistrations, sales. Turn on Only new and schedule it daily for a lead feed.
  2. Search by name. Look companies up in France (with officers), Norway, Finland, Estonia, Czechia, New York, Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon and, for any other country, the LEI index.

Good to know

  • French announcements carry no industry code, so with an industry filter each company is looked up in the French directory first: a day of announcements takes a few minutes. Keywords or places narrow the list first and make it faster.
  • Records are as the registers publish them; url opens the official page.
  • US records: New York and Oregon list active businesses; the New York address is the one the state serves legal papers to (usually the company's). Colorado, Connecticut and Oregon name the registered agent.
  • Officers come from the registers as published; switch them off with includeOfficers: false.

Questions

Questions about Company Registry

How much does Company Registry cost?

$2.50 per 1,000, charged only for companys returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Company Registry return?

Every row has the same fields, including name, registryId, country, event, eventDate, eventDetail, legalForm, status, registeredOn, industryCode, industry, address, employees, capital, officers, management, email, website, url. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as find_companies.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
