How Auctions Radar works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Foreclosure, court, tax and bank auctions from Germany, Spain, Italy and India in one clean format: price, valuation, debts on the property, area, price per m², possession, dates, deposit, documents and photos. Every lot is scored against similar lots nearby and against your budget, place and dates, with plain-word reasons, price history and new-lot alerts.

Built for property investors and flippers, auction buyers and brokers, valuers and lenders, distressed-asset funds, relocation and buying agents, car and equipment traders, researchers and AI agents.

How the scoring works

  • Compared with its peers. A flat in Milan is measured against other flats in Milan, a Tata truck against other Tata trucks. When a town has too few lots, the region or country is used, and the output says which group was used and how many lots were in it.
  • Never across markets. Lots are only compared within the same country, currency and kind of asset, so a Mumbai flat never sets the bar for a Berlin one.
  • Signals. Price per m², price, discount to valuation, how far the reserve has been cut, possession, clear title (no debts carried over), how much information and paperwork is published, time to the auction, deposit size and bank financing. Vehicles use year, mileage and condition.
  • Your requirements. Budget (with optional flex), distance from your place, area and auction window give a fit score. fitWeight sets how much fit counts against quality (default 30%).
  • Profiles. balanced, bargainHunter (cheapest for what you get), lowRisk (vacant, clear title, full paperwork) and quickDeal (soon, vacant, financing). Or set your own weights per signal.
  • Honest about gaps. A lot with missing fields is scored on what is known, and scoring.coverage shows how much was known.

Alerts and monitoring

  • Only new. Each run returns only lots earlier runs did not deliver.
  • Changes only. With a monitorName, each run returns new lots and lots whose price, date or status changed, with changeType and priceHistory.
  • Schedule it and send results to Slack, email, Google Sheets or a webhook.

Pricing

Good to know

  • Prices, valuations and dates are what the official portal shows when the run reads it. Always read the official notice and documents before you bid.
  • German lots show the court's valuation; the lowest accepted first bid is half of it.
  • Spanish lots can carry debts that stay with the property. totalCost adds them to the price, and the score counts them.
  • Indian lots leave out the names of borrowers and owners.
  • This Actor reads public auction listings only. It does not bid, register or sign in.

Questions

Questions about Auctions Radar

How much does Auctions Radar cost?

$15 per 1,000, charged only for lots returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Auctions Radar return?

Every row has the same fields, including title, url, image, images, documents, source, sourceName, country, seller, saleType, caseNumber, lotNumber, kind, category, description, address, city, district, region, postcode, latitude, longitude, distanceKm, price, minimumBid, valuation, currency, priceConverted, encumbrances, totalCost, discountToValuation, areaSqm, pricePerSqm, rooms, auctionStart, auctionDate, daysLeft, status, depositDeadline. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as find_property_auctions.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
