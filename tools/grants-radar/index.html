Guide

How Grants Radar works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Open and upcoming public funding calls in one clean schema: US federal grants from every agency (Grants.gov), EU programmes (Horizon Europe, Digital Europe, LIFE, EU4Health, Erasmus+, CEF and cascade calls on the Funding & Tenders portal) and UK Research and Innovation. Filter by keyword, sector, grant size and deadline; add AI insights that tell you what is funded, who can apply and how well each call fits you.

Built for grant writers and research offices, startups and SMEs, non-profits, universities, funding consultancies, and AI agents that find funding for their users.

Why this Actor

  • One schema for every funder. title, funder, programme, status (open / forecast), opens, deadline, awardMin, awardMax, totalFunding, expectedAwards, eligibility, sectors, url.
  • What needs action first. Results come soonest deadline first; filter by grant size and by how many days you need before the deadline.
  • Plan ahead. Forecast calls (announced, not yet open) are included by default.
  • AI insights (optional). An AI reads each call: summary, what is funded, who can apply, money and deadline as written, and, with your profile, a 0-100 fit score with the reason. Use "Apify (no key)" or your own key.
  • Funding alerts. Turn on Only new calls and schedule it weekly; send results to Slack, Google Sheets, a webhook or an RSS reader.
  • For AI agents. Callable as an MCP tool.

Pricing

$3 per 1,000 calls plus a small start fee per run. AI insights, when you turn them on, are $0.01 each, plus the model's tokens: about $0.0003 per insight with the default model (billed to your Apify account by Apify's AI gateway), or your provider's price with your own key. Calls that don't match your filters, and (with Only new calls) calls already delivered, are free.

Good to know

Amounts are in each call's currency (USD, EUR). Always check eligibility and conditions on the official page before applying. More national portals are being added.

Questions

Questions about Grants Radar

How much does Grants Radar cost?

$3 per 1,000, charged only for grant calls returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Grants Radar return?

Every row has the same fields, including deadline, funder, title, status, awardMax, totalFunding, sectors, programme. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as search_grants.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
