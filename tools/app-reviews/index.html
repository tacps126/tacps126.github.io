Guide

How App Store & Google Play Reviews Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Get app reviews from the Apple App Store and Google Play in one clean dataset - rating, title, text, author, date, app version and the developer's reply - with sentiment, reply time and the app's overall rating added for you. Track your own app, benchmark competitors, or mine complaints for product ideas. Export to JSON, CSV or Excel, or send new reviews to Slack automatically.

Built for product managers and app developers, customer-support and community teams, app marketers and ASO specialists, and analysts comparing apps.

Why this scraper

  • Both stores, one format. Paste App Store and Google Play links side by side; every review comes back with the same fields, so iOS and Android feedback sit in one table.
  • Ready-to-analyze. Each review has sentiment (from the stars), textLength, hasReply and replyDelayDays - plus the app's name, developer and overall rating - no spreadsheet work needed.
  • Exactly the reviews you need. Filter by star rating (e.g. 1-2 ★), date, words mentioned (crash, login), review length, and whether the developer replied - the Actor stops as soon as it has enough matching reviews.
  • Fast and cheap to run. Runs finish in seconds: hundreds of reviews in a few seconds, for a fraction of a cent of platform usage.
  • More App Store reviews with countries. The App Store keeps reviews per country; add us, gb, de, in... to collect each country's reviews in one run.
  • Review alerts built in. Schedule it with Only new reviews and get just the reviews you haven't seen - perfect for a daily Slack digest.
  • Handles rate limits for you. Waits as long as a store asks when it says "slow down" (HTTP 429), and retries flaky responses automatically.

How to use it

  1. Paste app links into Apps - for example https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spotify-music-and-podcasts/id324684580 and https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spotify.music.
  2. Optionally add Countries and Google Play languages, pick a sort order, star ratings, a start date, words to look for, or unanswered only.
  3. Press Start. Download from the Output tab, or connect an integration.

Review alerts on a schedule

Turn on Only new reviews and schedule the Actor (hourly or daily). Each run returns only reviews earlier runs haven't delivered - you pay only for new ones. Connect Slack, e-mail, Google Sheets, Zapier or Make to route them to your team.

Use it as an instant API

The Actor also runs in Standby mode - an always-ready endpoint that returns reviews straight in the response. Find the URL on the Standby tab:

Questions

Questions about App Store & Google Play Reviews Scraper

How many App Store reviews can I get?

Apple shows up to about 500 of the most recent (or most helpful) reviews per country - sometimes fewer. Add more countries to collect more.

How many Google Play reviews?

Up to 10,000 per app and run, newest or most relevant first.

Is it legal?

The Actor collects publicly posted reviews. Handle reviewer names responsibly and follow the privacy rules that apply to you.

How much does App Store & Google Play Reviews Scraper cost?

$0.40 per 1,000, charged only for reviews returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does App Store & Google Play Reviews Scraper return?

Every row has the same fields, including store, appId / appName / developer / appUrl / appIcon, appRating / appRatingCount, country / language, reviewId / rating / title / text, author / authorUrl / authorImageUrl, date, appVersion. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as get_app_reviews.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
