Innovation Radar — Field Trends & Idea Validation

Analyse a technology field or validate a product idea from open research and patent data: growth and momentum, leading countries, institutions and funders, rising sub-topics, standout and closest work, related patents. Optional AI opportunities on your own key. $0.03 per analysis.

Change any field; the code on the right follows. Copy it, or run the same input on Apify.

Landscape: size, growth, leaders, funders and rising topics of a field. Validate: how new or crowded an idea is, and the closest prior work.

For landscapes, e.g. `solid state battery`, `CRISPR base editing`, `retrieval augmented generation`. Multi-word fields are searched as a phrase.

For validation, in plain words, e.g. `a window that generates solar power with perovskite tandem cells`.

How far back the trend goes.

Standout recent works (landscape) or closest works (validation).

AI analysis (optional)

Add opportunities (what to build, for whom, why now, capital needed, who to talk to) or an idea assessment, written by the AI below. Without it you still get every score and signal, plus an AI_BRIEF you can paste into any assistant.

Language of the AI insight. Default English.
