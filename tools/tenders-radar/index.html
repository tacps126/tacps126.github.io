Tenders Radar — Public Tenders & Contract Awards

Public tenders and awards from 17 official portals in one schema: US SAM.gov and USAspending, EU TED and EU institutions, UK, France, Spain, Germany, India GeM, Brazil, Ukraine, Canada, Colombia, Australia, South Africa. Country, state, sector, value filters. From $4/1k.

Words or phrases matched on title and description (whole words). They match literally, so add translations for non-English portals (e.g. software, logiciel, Software), or use sectors and codes, which work in every language.

Company names: lists the contracts each one won (US federal, EU/EEA via TED, UK Contracts Finder) with a summary per company, instead of searching open tenders. Use it for competitor tracking, sales prospecting and due diligence.

How far back to list a supplier's contract wins.

ISO codes or names: CA, GB, FR, DE, ES, NL, CO, AU, ZA, or EU for every EU/EEA country on TED. Leave empty for all.

AI insights (optional)

Add an `insight` to each notice: a plain-language summary, key points, what to do and by when, and more. With a profile, also a 0-100 fit score and its reason.

What your organisation does and looks for, e.g. "We build and maintain cloud data platforms for public bodies in Germany and Austria; contracts above €100k". Each insight then scores how well the notice fits.

Insights are written in this language, whatever the source language.

Insights are added to the first N notices (most relevant first); the rest are delivered without one.

Monitoring

Schedule the Actor (daily) and each run returns only notices earlier runs didn't deliver: a bid-alert feed you pay for only when something new appears.

What you get back

The fields every row has.

FieldWhat it holds
title, description, referenceAs published; reference is the buyer's own number
buyer{name, id, city, region, country}; for SAM.gov the name is department / agency / office
contactsThe buyer's published contact points {name, email, phone}
country, subdivisionISO code of the buyer's country; ISO 3166-2 state or region where the work is or the buyer sits (US-TX), when known
statusopen, closed, awarded, planned, cancelled, unknown
noticeTypetender, award, planning, amendment, cancellation
category, procedureWorks / goods / services; procedure as published (for SAM.gov, the set-aside, e.g. Total Small Business Set-Aside)
classifications, sectorsCPV / UNSPSC / NAICS / PSC codes and the sectors they map to
value{amount, currency} (estimated value, or the awarded amount)
publishedAt, deadline, awardDateRFC 3339
suppliersWinners, for awards
documents, urlNotice documents and the official notice page
