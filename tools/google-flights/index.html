Guide

How Google Flights Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Get flight fares from Google Flights for any routes and dates as a clean dataset: price, airlines, departure and arrival times, total duration, stops and layovers (airport and minutes), every leg's flight number, aircraft and legroom, plus CO2 emissions against the typical flight. One-way or round trip, economy to first, any currency. Export to JSON, CSV or Excel, send to Google Sheets, or call it as an API.

Built for travel startups and agencies comparing fares, analysts tracking airfare by route and season, corporate travel teams, and deal hunters building fare calendars.

Why this scraper

  • Many routes at once. Every origin to every destination on every date you list.
  • Fare calendars. List 30 dates and get the fares for each day.
  • Round trips. Set a trip length in days; prices are for the whole trip.
  • Any cabin and currency. Economy, premium economy, business or first; USD, EUR, INR and more.
  • Flight details. Flight numbers, aircraft, legroom, layover airports and times.
  • Best flights marked. Google's "best" options flagged.

How to use it

  1. Add From, To (airport codes or cities) and Departure dates (2026-12-15, or +30 for 30 days from today, which keeps scheduled runs current).
  2. Optionally set a round trip length, cabin, max stops and currency.
  3. Press Start. Download results from the Output tab, or connect an integration.

Pricing

On Apify's free plan each run returns up to 100 flights. Any paid Apify plan removes the limit.

Questions

Questions about Google Flights Scraper

How many options per search?

About 15 to 25, the list Google Flights shows for a route and date.

Round-trip return flights?

Round-trip searches return the outbound options with the price of the whole trip.

Booking links?

Not yet. Open an issue if you need them.

Is it legal?

The Actor collects publicly shown fares. You're responsible for how you use the data under the laws that apply to you.

How much does Google Flights Scraper cost?

$1 per 1,000, charged only for flights returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.
