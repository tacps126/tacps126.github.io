Guide

How Restaurant Inspections Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Restaurant and food establishment inspections from US cities and counties, in one format. Each inspection comes with the business, address, inspection type, result, score or grade, and every violation cited, read from the health department's own official open-data table.

At a glance

  • Areas: New York City, New York State (outside NYC), Chicago, Austin, King County (Seattle), Montgomery County (MD), Cincinnati, Delaware and Marin County (CA). Any other city or county that publishes its food inspections on an open-data site: paste the table's address.
  • One row per inspection: tables that publish one row per violation are merged, so each inspection carries its full list of violations (code, description, and whether it was critical where the area marks it) and a count.
  • Filters: inspected in the last N days, keywords in the business name, facility type, cuisine, result or violations (fail, mice, temperature, school...), words to leave out.
  • Monitoring: turn on Only new since last run and put the run on a schedule to get each new inspection once.
  • Price: $2 per 1,000 inspections, plus a small start fee per run. Empty runs cost only the start fee.

Who uses it

  • Pest control, cleaning, food safety training and equipment companies looking for places that just failed or were cited.
  • Restaurant suppliers, delivery and POS sales teams building lists of active food businesses.
  • Journalists, local news sites and apps that publish inspection results.
  • Insurers, landlords and investors checking a place's record.

Questions

Questions about Restaurant Inspections Scraper

How fresh is the data?

As fresh as each health department's table. Most update daily or weekly; Austin updates about once a month, so use a longer window there.

Is this personal data?

Inspection results are public records about businesses. Use them within the law that applies to you.

How much does Restaurant Inspections Scraper cost?

$2 per 1,000, charged only for inspections returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Restaurant Inspections Scraper return?

Every row has the same fields, including jurisdiction, inspectionDate, businessName, facilityType, inspectionType, result, score, grade. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
