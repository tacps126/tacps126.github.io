Guide

How Levels.fyi Salary Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Compensation from Levels.fyi for any company, job family and location: total, base, stock and bonus for every level (L3, L4, Senior, Staff...), with submissions, years of experience, p10-p90 percentiles, the median offer and the company's profile. Or pull a job family's whole market (top-paying companies and locations) or a company's pay across all its job families.

Built for recruiters and talent teams setting pay bands, compensation analysts, job seekers preparing to negotiate, HR-tech and salary tools, and AI agents.

Why this Actor

  • Every location. Countries and city areas (India, United Kingdom, New York City Area, Bangalore...), with pay in local currency.
  • More than the level averages. Percentiles, median offer, typical experience, workforce share, other titles and the company profile in every row, at no extra charge.
  • Three views of the same data: by level, by market, by company.
  • AI insights (optional). Where each level's pay sits, a negotiation tip and how much data backs it; with your profile, a fit score.
  • For AI agents. Callable as an MCP tool.

How to use it

Pay by level at three companies, in the US and India:

json
{
  "companies": ["google", "meta", "microsoft"],
  "jobFamilies": ["software-engineer"],
  "locations": ["united-states", "india"]
}

Top-paying companies for product managers in the UK:

json
{ "mode": "market", "jobFamilies": ["product-manager"], "locations": ["united-kingdom"] }

Every job family at a company:

json
{ "mode": "company", "companies": ["stripe", "openai"] }
