Guide

How Recalls Radar works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Product recalls and safety alerts from seven regulators in one clean feed: US FDA enforcement reports (food, drugs and medical devices), the US Consumer Product Safety Commission, Health Canada, CFIA and Transport Canada, the UK Food Standards Agency and Office for Product Safety and Standards, and the EU Safety Gate (dangerous non-food products across the EU and EEA). Filter by your brands, suppliers or product types; get only what's new; let AI say what's affected and what to do.

Built for retailers and marketplaces (pull recalled listings fast), brands and manufacturers (watch your category and competitors), importers and distributors, product-liability insurers, food safety and quality teams, and AI agents.

Free weekly dataset: the last 7 days of recalls on Hugging Face, no account needed.

Why this Actor

  • One schema. category, title, product, brands, company, hazard, classification, date, status, distribution, quantity, remedy, madeIn, image, url.
  • Watch what matters. Keywords match the title, product, brand, company and hazard: peanut, lithium battery, your brand or your supplier.
  • Recall alerts. Turn on Only new and schedule it daily; send results to Slack, email, Google Sheets, a webhook or an RSS reader.
  • AI insights (optional). What exactly is affected, how serious it is, what sellers should do, and with your profile a relevance score.
  • For AI agents. Callable as an MCP tool.

Questions

Questions about Recalls Radar

How much does Recalls Radar cost?

$2 per 1,000, charged only for recalls returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Recalls Radar return?

Every row has the same fields, including date, country, category, title, company, hazard, classification, url. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as search_recalls.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
