Guide

How Amazon Product Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Get Amazon products by search term or link as a clean dataset: price, list price, rating, number of ratings, "bought in past month", sponsored flag, badge and image for every search result, on 14 Amazon marketplaces. Turn on full details to also get the brand, feature bullets, specifications, all images, best sellers rank, seller, availability and star breakdown from each product page. Export to JSON, CSV or Excel, send to Google Sheets, or call it as an API.

Built for Amazon sellers and brands watching competitors, price trackers, market researchers sizing a category, and agencies building product catalogs.

Why this scraper

  • Two price points. Search results at $1 per 1,000; full product details only when you want them.
  • Sales signal. "Bought in past month" counts where Amazon shows them.
  • Sponsored marked. Keep or skip sponsored results.
  • Your own searches. Paste an Amazon search link with your filters and sorting.
  • 14 marketplaces. US, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Canada, India, Japan, Australia, Mexico, Brazil, Netherlands, UAE, in local currency.
  • Filters. Minimum rating, maximum price.

How to use it

  1. Add Search terms, or Product or search links (product links, ASINs, Amazon search links).
  2. Pick the Marketplace, the number of pages per search and the Sort (featured, price, reviews, newest, best sellers); set a price range or minimum rating if you like.
  3. Turn on Full product details if you need specs, images and seller.
  4. Press Start. Download results from the Output tab, or connect an integration.

Questions

Questions about Amazon Product Scraper

Does it return reviews?

It returns the rating, ratings count and star breakdown. Review texts are not included.

Why do some products have no price?

Amazon shows no price for some listings (unavailable, or only from other sellers).

Can I track prices over time?

Yes. Schedule the run daily and compare rows by asin.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as get_amazon_products.
