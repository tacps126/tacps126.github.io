Guide

How Market Pulse works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Give it a watchlist and get one row per ticker that says what moved and why: a one-line summary, alerts ranked by level, and the evidence behind them. Unusual price moves and volume, signals that fired today with how they worked on this ticker before, news and crowd sentiment, insider buying and selling, new SEC filings, short interest and the next earnings date. US, European and Japanese stocks. No API key, no account.

Schedule it on trading days and send only the tickers that need a look to Slack, email or Google Sheets.

How to use

  1. Add your watchlist (AAPL, NVDA, 7203.T, SAP.DE, HSBA.L, MC.PA, or NASDAQ:AAPL).
  2. Optional: return only tickers with alerts at a minimum level, or only those whose pulse changed since the last run.
  3. Press Start, or schedule it for every trading day and connect Slack, email, Google Sheets or a webhook.

{
  "tickers": ["AAPL", "NVDA", "TSLA", "7203.T", "SAP.DE"],

Tickers filtered out by the alert level or the only-changes option are free. A daily pulse on 20 tickers costs about $0.165; with a minimum alert level it is usually less.

Good to know

  • Alerts and scores describe prices, news and filings. They are not investment advice, and past signals do not promise future moves.
  • Insider trades, filings and short interest cover US listings. Other markets get the price, technical, news and sentiment parts.
  • Short interest is published twice a month, so it changes only on those dates.
  • Prices can be delayed by up to 15-20 minutes during market hours.
Questions

Questions about Market Pulse

How is this different from Stock Market Data & Technical Analysis?

That Actor returns the full analysis (every indicator, level and backtest) per ticker. Market Pulse reads it for you, adds insiders, filings, short interest and wider news, and tells you which tickers need a look today.

What does "only changes" compare against?

The last run with the same watchlist (or the same Monitor name).

How much does Market Pulse cost?

$8 per 1,000, charged only for ticker pulses returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Market Pulse return?

Every row has the same fields, including summary, alertLevel, alerts, bias, score, scores, move, session, technical, sentiment, insiders. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as get_market_pulse.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
