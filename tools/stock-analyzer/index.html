Guide

How Stock Analyzer works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Paste tickers and get a direct answer for each one, not a wall of indicators: is the chart bullish, neutral or bearish, how sure is that read, why, and which price levels matter. Stocks, ETFs, indices and crypto in the US, Europe and Japan. No API key, no account. Works well from AI agents and chatbots: one short row per ticker.

How to use

  1. Add tickers as on Yahoo Finance (AAPL, SPY, 7203.T, SAP.DE, HSBA.L, BTC-USD) or TradingView (NASDAQ:AAPL).
  2. Press Start. Export to JSON, CSV or Excel, or send to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. Schedule it daily for a watchlist.

Good to know

  • The verdict is a technical read of the chart, with news and crowd mood as one input. It is not investment advice, and past patterns do not promise future moves.
  • Confidence says how much the pieces agree (rating, trend on three timeframes, momentum, sentiment); a "leaning" verdict is never high confidence.
  • Prices can be delayed by up to 15-20 minutes during market hours.
Questions

Questions about Stock Analyzer

How much does Stock Analyzer cost?

$2 per 1,000, charged only for stock answers returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Stock Analyzer return?

Every row has the same fields, including verdict, confidence, answer, why, against, trend, momentum, rsi14, support, resistance, stopIfLong, stopIfShort, range5Days. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as analyze_stocks.
