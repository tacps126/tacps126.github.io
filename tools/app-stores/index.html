Guide

How App Store and Google Play Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Get apps from the App Store and Google Play in one table: rating, ratings count, installs, price, developer (with support email and website), genre, release and last update dates, what's new, description, icon and screenshots. Find apps by search term, by link, or from the App Store top free and top paid charts. Export to JSON, CSV or Excel, send to Google Sheets, or call it as an API.

Built for app marketers and ASO teams watching competitors, investors and analysts tracking app categories, agencies building lead lists of app developers, and developers checking their own listings across countries.

Why this scraper

  • Both stores, one row shape. Same field names for App Store and Google Play, so one sheet covers both.
  • Search, links or charts. Mix search terms, app links and top charts in one run.
  • Ratings in detail. Average rating, number of ratings, Google Play reviews count and star breakdown.
  • Installs. Google Play install band (1,000,000+) and the exact install count the store reports.
  • Developer contacts. Google Play support email, website and privacy policy link.
  • Any country. Prices, ratings and charts for the country you pick.
  • Filters. Free apps only, minimum rating.

How to use it

  1. Add Search terms, Apps (links, App Store ids or package names) or pick an App Store top chart.
  2. Optionally choose stores, country and filters.
  3. Press Start. Download results from the Output tab, or connect an integration.

Questions

Questions about App Store and Google Play Scraper

Does it return reviews?

No, this one returns app details. For reviews use App Reviews Scraper.

Why is an app missing for my country?

Some apps are not offered in every country's store; the run log names them.

Can I track ratings over time?

Yes. Schedule the run daily or weekly and compare rows by appId.

Are Google Play charts included?

Not in this version; App Store top free and top paid are.

How much does App Store and Google Play Scraper cost?

$0.60 per 1,000, charged only for apps returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as search_app_stores.
