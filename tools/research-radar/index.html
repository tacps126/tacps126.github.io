Guide

How Research Radar works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Search OpenAlex, arXiv, PubMed, Europe PMC and worldwide patents (European Patent Office) at once and get one clean, deduplicated, newest-first list of papers, preprints and patents - with abstracts, authors and affiliations, DOIs, PDF links, citation counts and open-access status. Schedule it with Only new results and it becomes a research alert; follow it as an RSS/Atom feed; add AI summaries and multilingual search on your own AI key.

Built for R&D and innovation teams watching a technology, IP and patent professionals tracking prior art, pharma and biotech scouting new findings, investors and analysts following a field, librarians, and AI agents that need fresh research.

Why this radar

  • Many sources, one record each. The same paper found by OpenAlex, PubMed and Europe PMC comes back once, with the best fields from each and a foundIn list. Matching uses DOIs, PubMed / PMC / arXiv ids and normalized titles.
  • Fresh first. Results are sorted newest first; filter by Published within N days or a start date.
  • Research alerts. Schedule it with Only new results: each run returns only what earlier runs didn't deliver, so you pay only for new papers.
  • RSS / Atom feed. Call the Actor's Standby URL with &format=atom and paste it into Feedly, Slack, Teams, Inoreader or Zapier. Repeat polls within 30 minutes are served from memory - no new search, no new charge.
  • AI summaries and multilingual search (optional). Get a short plain-language summary of each result, or search your query translated into other languages (e.g. German, Japanese, Chinese). Use Apify's built-in AI (no key; a fraction of a cent per call, billed to your Apify account) or your own Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek or local model. The Actor charges nothing extra for AI.
  • Influence, not just volume. Each paper carries its citations per year, a field-normalized impact (1.0 = the world average for papers of the same field and year) and a top 1% / top 10% flag. Sort by Most cited or Highest impact to find the work that matters.
  • Retractions left out. Retracted papers are dropped by default (or kept and flagged, if you ask).
  • Ready to share and cite. Every run saves a readable digest (REPORT: newest and most influential per query) and CITATIONS.bib for Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote or LaTeX.
  • Official open sources only. Fast, reliable runs.

How to use it

  1. Enter one or more search queries (solid state battery, CRISPR base editing, "retrieval augmented generation").
  2. Pick sources (all five are on by default) and optionally Published within (days).
  3. Press Start. For alerts, turn on Only new results and add a schedule (daily or weekly).

Example input:

json
{
  "queries": ["solid state battery", "sulfide electrolyte"],
  "postedWithinDays": 30,
  "maxItems": 200,
  "onlyNew": true
}

Pricing

$3 per 1,000 results (each paper or patent counts once, however many sources found it) plus a small start fee per run. Duplicates and, with Only new results, already-delivered items are free. AI summaries and translations cost the model's tokens: a fraction of a cent each with the built-in AI (billed to your Apify account by Apify's AI gateway), or your provider's price with your own key. See the Pricing tab, and set a maximum charge per run to cap spend.

Tips

  • Use several specific queries rather than one broad one; results are merged.
  • Queries match titles and abstracts, so papers that only mention your words in passing stay out.
  • For a literature review, set Sort by to Highest impact with Published within 1,100 days: the most influential work of the last three years, deduplicated, with BibTeX.
  • For a weekly alert, set Published within to 8 days and schedule weekly with Only new results.
  • A free OpenAlex key gives your runs their own daily budget.

Questions

Questions about Research Radar

How much does Research Radar cost?

$3 per 1,000, charged only for results returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Research Radar return?

Every row has the same fields, including type, title, abstract, authors, publishedAt, venue, doi, url, pdfUrl, openAccess, citations, language, keywords, citationsPerYear. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as search_research.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
