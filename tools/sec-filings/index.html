Guide

How SEC Filings and Insider Trading Scraper (EDGAR, Form 4) works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Company filings and insider trades from the US Securities and Exchange Commission, as clean rows. Give it tickers; get the documents each company filed, or every trade its officers, directors and large shareholders reported.

At a glance

  • Filings: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, S-1, proxy statements, 13F and every other form, with links to the document and the filing page.
  • Insider trades: one row per trade from Form 4: who traded, their role, purchase or sale, shares, price, value and shares held afterwards.
  • Monitoring: turn on Only new since last run and schedule it to follow a watchlist.
  • Price: $0.40 per 1,000 filings, $2 per 1,000 insider trades, plus a small start fee per run.
  • Source: the SEC's own public data.

Who uses it

  • Investors and analysts following a watchlist for new reports and insider buying.
  • Fintech and research products that need filings or insider activity as data.
  • Sales and account research teams watching for 8-K events at target companies.

Questions

Questions about SEC Filings and Insider Trading Scraper (EDGAR, Form 4)

Which trades count as purchases and sales?

Open-market purchases (code P) and sales (code S). Awards, option exercises, gifts and shares withheld for tax are returned under All with their own label.

How far back does it go?

The most recent filings of each company, usually the last one to two years for active filers.

Can I look up a fund or a private filer?

Yes, by its CIK number.

Is this investment advice?

No. It is public filing data.

How much does SEC Filings and Insider Trading Scraper (EDGAR, Form 4) cost?

$0.40 per 1,000, charged only for filings returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does SEC Filings and Insider Trading Scraper (EDGAR, Form 4) return?

Every row has the same fields, including ticker, company, form, filedDate, insiderName, transaction, shares, pricePerShare. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
