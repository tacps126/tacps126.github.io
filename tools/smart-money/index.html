Guide

How Smart Money Tracker works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

See what famous investors bought and sold, who owns a big piece of a stock, and whether company insiders are buying. Everything comes from official SEC filings. No API key, no account.

  • Fund portfolios (13F): type a fund or a manager (Warren Buffett, Michael Burry, Cathie Wood) and get its holdings with the change against the previous quarter: new, added, reduced, sold out, and the estimated dollar flow.
  • Big stakes (13D/13G): for any US stock, every investor who reported owning more than 5%, activist or passive, with percent of class, shares, event date and the stated purpose.
  • Insider buying: one row per stock that sums up open-market buys and sales by officers and directors and flags cluster buying (3 or more insiders buying within two weeks).
  • Congress trades (news): stock trades US House members report under the STOCK Act, as public-interest news: member, district, asset, ticker, purchase or sale, owner (self, spouse, joint), amount range, trade and filing dates, how many days they took to disclose.
  • Unusual options activity: per stock, call and put volume, contracts where volume is well above open interest (new positions) with the premium paid, a call-heavy or put-heavy signal, and max pain for the nearest expiry.
  • Futures positioning (CFTC COT): for gold, oil, the S&P 500, Treasuries, currencies, bitcoin and more, how speculators and hedgers are positioned, the weekly change, and the COT index (where the speculators' net position sits in its 3-year range; extremes are classic contrarian signals).
  • India FII/DII flows: foreign and domestic institutions' net buying in the Indian cash market each day, with streaks and 5 and 20-day totals.

Visual report

Turn on Visual report and the run also saves one page with charts, linked from the run's Output tab: each fund's top positions by weight and its biggest buys and sells of the quarter, insider net buying per ticker, holders above 5%, Congress trades (purchases vs sales, most traded tickers), options flow (calls vs puts, premium in unusual contracts, max pain), futures positioning with the COT index and weekly history, and India's daily institutional flows. It is built from the rows the run already collected.

Good to know

  • Funds file 13F reports up to 45 days after each quarter ends (mid February, May, August, November). Run with holdings: changes after those dates to see the new moves. 13F covers US-listed long positions and options only; shorts and cash are not reported.
  • Some funds stop filing or file late; their rows carry staleReport: true and the run log says so.
  • Schedule 13D/13G filings before late 2024 are listed with a link only (no percent or shares).
  • Congress trades come from the House Clerk's reports; reports filed on paper (scanned) are skipped and counted in the run summary. Amounts are the ranges members report, not exact values.
  • Options volumes are from the last trading session. COT reports come out on Fridays for positions as of Tuesday.
  • NSE publishes FII/DII figures for the latest day only, so streaks and multi-day totals grow as you run the Actor on a schedule.
  • Insider rows count open-market purchases and sales (Form 4 codes P and S), not grants or option exercises.

Congress trades are news

The Congress rows report what lawmakers disclosed, so readers, journalists and researchers can follow it. Federal law (5 U.S.C. 13107) does not allow using congressional financial disclosure reports for a commercial purpose, other than by news and communications media reporting to the general public, or to set anyone's credit rating or to raise money. Use these rows to inform and report, not to sell the reports themselves or to contact the people named. Each row links to the official report.

Questions about Smart Money Tracker

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.
