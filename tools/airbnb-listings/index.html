Guide

How Airbnb Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Get Airbnb stays for any city, area or neighborhood and your travel dates as a clean, spreadsheet-ready dataset. Each stay comes with its name, type of place, total price for your dates and price per night, the original price when it is discounted, rating and review count, bedrooms, beds and baths, the area, GPS coordinates, the Guest favorite badge, the highlight line and photos, plus a link that opens the stay for your dates. Export to JSON, CSV or Excel, send to Google Sheets, or call it as an API.

Built for short-term rental investors sizing up a market, property managers benchmarking their prices, revenue managers tracking discounts, travel startups, and analysts studying supply and pricing by area.

Why this scraper

  • Plain-word places. Type Lisbon, Portugal, Austin, TX or Shoreditch, London. Or paste any Airbnb search link with your filters.
  • Real prices for real dates. Set check-in and check-out to get the total for the stay, the nightly rate and the pre-discount price.
  • Ratings and reviews. Average rating and number of reviews for every stay; new stays are flagged.
  • Filters. Guests, type of place (entire home, private room, shared room), price range per night, minimum bedrooms, currency.
  • Map-ready. Latitude and longitude on every stay.
  • New-listing alerts. Schedule it with Only new stays and each run returns only stays you haven't received yet, so you pay only for new ones.

How to use it

  1. Add Locations (e.g. Lisbon, Portugal) and optionally Check-in and Check-out dates.
  2. Optionally set Adults, Type of place, a price range and Min bedrooms.
  3. Press Start. Download results from the Output tab, or connect an integration.

Questions

Questions about Airbnb Scraper

How many stays can I get per search?

Airbnb shows up to 15 pages of about 18 stays for one search. For more, split a city into neighborhoods or price bands, or add more locations.

Are prices per night or for the whole stay?

Both: price.amount is what Airbnb shows for your dates, and price.perNight divides it by the nights. Without dates, Airbnb shows its own default dates.

Can I get the host, amenities or reviews text?

Not yet. Open an issue if you need them.

Is it legal?

The Actor collects publicly listed stays. You're responsible for how you use the data under the laws that apply to you.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Airbnb Scraper return?

Every row has the same fields, including id, url, name, title, propertyType, highlight, area, latitude, longitude, price, priceText, rating, reviewsCount, isNew, bedrooms, beds, bathrooms, facts, guestFavorite, badges. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
