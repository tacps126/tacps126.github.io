Guide

How Hiring Signals & Buying Intent Data from Job Postings works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Know which companies are about to buy. When a company opens roles for the team you sell to - data engineers, security, RevOps, support - it's usually expanding that team and about to spend on its tools. This Actor watches your target accounts' career pages (or discovers companies through job searches) and gives you one scored signal per company: how many matching roles, how fresh, which functions, seniority and locations, and what's new since last week.

Built for SDRs and account executives prioritising outreach, RevOps teams feeding intent data into the CRM, founders doing account-based selling, recruiters and agencies spotting growing teams, and investors tracking portfolio and competitor growth.

At a glance

  • Price: 1 cent per company with matching roles; companies without them are free. Free plan: up to 50 companies per run.
  • First run: the prefilled input checks Stripe, Spotify and Ramp for data and machine-learning roles in about 3 seconds.
  • Sample data: results from a recent run as CSV or JSON. Opens in your browser, no account needed.

Why this Actor

  • Signals, not job dumps. One row per company, ranked by a simple score (more matching roles, fresher roles, and new roles since last run score higher), with the matching roles listed underneath. Ready to hand to a sales team.
  • Your definition of intent. Choose the role keywords that matter for your product, and narrow by seniority, job function, work mode, employment type, region and recency. Require tech-stack mentions (e.g. roles that mention Snowflake) for technographic signals, and exclude existing customers.
  • Tracks change over time. Every signal shows new matching roles and the change in the company's total open roles since the previous run. With Only new or changed companies, a weekly schedule becomes an intent feed - you're charged only for changes.
  • Just paste the website. Give a company's website (stripe.com) or name and it finds the company's jobs board itself, and only accepts a board that links back to that website. Reads Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Personio, Teamtailor and more; a jobs page link works too. No API keys or logins.
  • Fast and cheap to run. Runs finish in seconds. A dozen target accounts take a few seconds.
  • Handles rate limits for you. Waits as long as a site asks when it says "slow down" (HTTP 429), and retries timeouts and dropped connections automatically.

How to use it

  1. Paste your target accounts' websites - for example stripe.com (a name or a jobs page link like https://jobs.lever.co/spotify works too). Or add job searches (like data engineer in Germany) to discover companies you don't know yet. You can combine both.
  2. Enter the roles that signal intent for you, and anything to ignore.
  3. Press Start. Then schedule it weekly with Only new or changed companies and connect Slack, Google Sheets or your CRM.

Weekly intent feed

Turn on Only new or changed companies and schedule the Actor weekly. The first run reports every company with a signal; after that you only get companies that are new or opened new matching roles. Connect Slack, e-mail, Google Sheets, HubSpot or Salesforce (via Zapier or Make) so your team sees them where they work.

Hiring and layoffs side by side

Turn on Add US layoff notices (WARN) to match each company against the official WARN lists of California, Colorado, Iowa, Maryland and Texas. US employers must file these notices before larger layoffs and closures, so a company that is hiring data engineers while filing a closure for a warehouse shows both in one record. Pick the states and how far back to look (default: a year). Matching uses the company name; no extra charge.

Questions

Questions about Hiring Signals & Buying Intent Data from Job Postings

Which companies work?

Those whose jobs live on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Personio, Teamtailor, Workable, Breezy, Rippling, BambooHR or Pinpoint, which covers most tech and growth companies. Give the website and the board is found for you; if a company runs its own in-house careers site, it comes back as no-job-board-found (not charged).

How is "new" decided?

The Actor remembers which matching roles it saw for each company in the previous run with the same input (or the same state key). Roles it hasn't seen before are new.

Is it legal?

The Actor reads publicly posted job listings. Use the results within the privacy and marketing rules that apply to you.

How much does Hiring Signals & Buying Intent Data from Job Postings cost?

$0.01 each, charged only for company signals returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does Hiring Signals & Buying Intent Data from Job Postings return?

Every row has the same fields, including company / companySlug / companyLogo, input / jobsPage / jobsPageFoundBy, status, signalScore, matchingRoles / newMatchingRoles, rolesPostedLast7Days, totalOpenRoles / openRolesChange, functions / seniority / topLocations. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The webdata-tools-mcp server exposes it to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients as find_hiring_companies.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
