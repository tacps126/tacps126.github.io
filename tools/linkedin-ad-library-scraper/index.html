Guide

How LinkedIn Ad Library Scraper works

Inputs, options and tips, from the tool's own documentation.

Get the ads a company runs on LinkedIn from the LinkedIn Ad Library, or every ad that mentions a keyword. Ad text, headline, image or video, call to action, landing page, who paid, dates, impressions, the share of impressions per country and the targeting used: language, location, company, job, audiences. No LinkedIn account needed.

At a glance

  • Find ads your way: company name as LinkedIn shows it, website domain, LinkedIn company page link, a search link copied from the Ad Library, or keywords in ad text.
  • Ad creative: text, headline, image or video cover, call to action, landing page, and the text of message ads. Thought-leader ads name the person whose post it is and their title.
  • Reach and targeting: impressions range, share of impressions per country, and each targeting option with whether the advertiser used it and the values LinkedIn names. LinkedIn publishes these for ads shown in the EU.
  • Who is behind the ad: company name, ID, page, logo, and who paid.
  • Filters: country, running now or all, date range (LinkedIn keeps the last year), ad type.
  • Monitoring: turn on Only new ads and schedule the run to get each new ad once.
  • Price, each part below the usual Store price: $0.50 per 1,000 ads, plus $1.30 per 1,000 for the ad's page with dates, impressions, countries and targeting (on by default, charged only when LinkedIn publishes them for the ad). A small start fee per run; lower prices on higher Apify plans.

Questions

Questions about LinkedIn Ad Library Scraper

Why do some ads have no dates, impressions or targeting?

LinkedIn publishes those for ads shown in the EU. Other ads show the creative, the company and who paid, and are not charged the extra.

Why did a company name find nothing?

The name must match the company name on LinkedIn. Try the website domain, the company page link, or a search link copied from the Ad Library.

Need Google, Meta or TikTok ads too?

Competitor Ads (tacps126/competitor-ads) reads all four libraries in one run, in the same row shape.

How much does LinkedIn Ad Library Scraper cost?

$0.50 per 1,000, charged only for linkedin ads returned; a run that finds nothing costs nothing. It runs on Apify, whose free plan includes monthly credit, so you can try it without paying.

Do I need to write code?

No. Fill in the form on Apify and press Start, schedule it, or send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook. For code, the input builder on this page writes the API call in curl, Python or JavaScript.

What does LinkedIn Ad Library Scraper return?

Every row has the same fields, including source, advertiserName, creator, format, headline, text, callToAction, landingUrl. Download as JSON, CSV, Excel or XML.

Can it run on a schedule and return only new results?

Yes. Schedule it on Apify (hourly, daily, weekly) and turn on the only-new option: each run then returns only what appeared since the last run with the same input.
