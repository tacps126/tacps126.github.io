Data sources and removal

Where the data in our files comes from, and how to have your company's details removed or corrected. Removals take effect in every file within 7 days.

Last updated 2026-11-13. Also: Terms of sale, Privacy, Refunds.

Where the data comes from

  • Trade show exhibitor lists: the exhibitor directory the organiser publishes for the show, which anyone can open. A company email or phone missing there is taken from the contact or imprint page of the company's own website.
  • Other datasets: official registers and records published by governments and public agencies, under their own terms of reuse.
  • Not used: pages behind a login or a paywall, personal social media profiles, or data bought from brokers. We do not collect information about private individuals.

What a file contains

Company name, booth and hall, website, the company's business email and phone, postal address, product categories, the company's social media pages and its own description. The free sample on each page leaves emails and phone numbers out.

Remove your company or an address

Send a removal request

Or email tacps126@gmail.com with the subject "Removal request" and the company name and website, or the email address or phone number to remove. No reason is needed.

  • We remove it from every file we sell within 7 days and keep it on a removal list, so it does not come back in later refreshes.
  • We confirm by email.
  • Copies that buyers already have cannot be recalled, but our terms of sale require buyers to contact companies lawfully and to stop when asked.
  • Organisers who want a show left out can write to us the same way.

Correct your details

Send the correct details to tacps126@gmail.com; they go into the next refresh.
