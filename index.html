FAQ

Before you start

How do I try a tool?

Open it on Apify and press Start. Apify's free plan includes monthly credit, enough to try every tool on real data.

What does it cost?

You pay per result, about $1 per 1,000 for most tools. Searches that find nothing are not charged, and every run can be capped at a spend limit you set.

What happens when a website changes?

The tools are run against their live sources on a schedule. When a site changes, the tool is fixed and a note goes into its changelog.

Can it read pages behind a login or a captcha?

No. The tools read public pages only and never solve captchas. A few accept your own sign-in for pages your account can see, and say plainly when a source is closed.

Which formats can I get?

JSON, CSV, Excel, XML or HTML from the run's dataset, a Google Sheet, Slack, Discord or any webhook, and only-new results on a schedule.
