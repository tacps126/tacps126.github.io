How to get the Automechanika Dubai 2026 exhibitor list with emails (in 2 minutes)

2026-10-28 · 3 min read · sales leadgeneration tradeshows automation

Automechanika Dubai runs 10-12 November 2026 at Expo City Dubai. More than 800 companies exhibit: parts makers, distributors, workshop-equipment brands, tyre and lubricant traders from the UAE, India, China, Turkey, Germany and beyond.

If you sell to the automotive aftermarket, that exhibitor list is the best lead list of the year. The show's website lets you browse it one company at a time. This guide gets you the whole list as a spreadsheet, with emails, phones, websites and booths, in about two minutes.

What you get

One row per exhibitor:

Company(exhibitor's legal name)
BoothS / 04742
Emailsales@...

City, countrySharjah, United Arab Emirates
Description, product categories...

In our run, 790 of 804 exhibitors came with an email address.

Step 1: open the tool

Go to Trade Show Exhibitor List Scraper and click Try for free. If you don't have an Apify account, create one free; no card needed. The free plan returns up to 200 exhibitors per show, enough to check the data.

Step 2: pick the show

In Exhibitions, choose Automechanika Dubai. Or switch to JSON and paste:

json
{
  "exhibitionList": ["automechanika-dubai"],
  "maxResults": 1000,
  "includeDetails": true
}

Leave Include contact details on: that's what adds email, phone and address.

Step 3: run and download

Press Start. When it finishes, open Output > Export and pick CSV or Excel. The Contacts view gives you a flat table ready for your CRM.

Cost on a paid plan: about $3 per 1,000 exhibitors, so the whole show costs less than a coffee.

Step 4: narrow it down

A few filters that work well in a spreadsheet:

  • Country = India (or your market): 23 Indian exhibitors this year, a short list to call first.
  • Country = United Arab Emirates: about 120 local traders and distributors, the buyers most exhibitors want to meet.
  • Description contains brake, filter, battery, lubricant: your product category only.

Step 5: book meetings before the show

The list is worth most before the doors open. Email the companies you want to meet with their booth number in the first line ("I'll be at Automechanika, could I stop by S / 04742 on the 11th?"). Short, specific, booth in the subject: reply rates are far higher than cold email without a reason.

Keep it fresh

Exhibitor lists keep growing until the show opens. Turn on Only new exhibitors and schedule the tool daily (Apify > Schedules): each run returns only companies added since the last one.

Send it to your tools automatically

  • Google Sheets: the tool's Integrations tab, or our ready n8n workflow (show > Google Sheet).
  • Clay: Import from Apify > Actor run, then enrich each exhibitor with your usual providers.
  • Make / Zapier: the Apify app > Run an Actor tacps126/exhibition-scraper > Get dataset items.
