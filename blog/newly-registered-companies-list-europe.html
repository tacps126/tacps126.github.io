A daily list of newly registered companies in France, Norway, Finland and Estonia, from the official registers

2026-11-02 · 2 min read · sales leadgeneration b2b automation

A company that registered this week needs an accountant, a bank account, insurance, payroll, a website, a phone plan and a point-of-sale system, and it has not chosen any of them yet. If you sell any of those, new registrations are the warmest list you can get. Lead vendors resell them weeks later. The registers publish them every day.

This guide gets you the companies registered since yesterday, straight from the official registers, in about three seconds.

Where the data comes from

  • France: BODACC, the official bulletin of company announcements
  • Norway: the Brønnøysund Register Centre
  • Finland: the PRH / YTJ business information system
  • Estonia: the e-Business Register
  • United States: the open business registers of New York, Colorado, Connecticut and Oregon

Insolvencies and other events are in the same feed for credit and collections teams.

Step 1: open the tool

Go to Company Registry and click Try for free. No Apify account? Create one free; no card needed. The free plan returns up to 100 companies per run.

Step 2: choose countries and filters

The prefilled input returns 40 companies registered this week across four countries. To focus, switch to JSON:

json
{
  "mode": "new",
  "countries": ["FI", "NO"],
  "registeredWithinDays": 2,
  "keywords": ["software", "consulting"]
}

Filter by industry codes or locations the same way.

Step 3: run and export

Click Start and export to Excel, CSV or Google Sheets.

Get it every morning

Add "onlyNew": true, "monitorName": "new-companies" and schedule the run daily: each run returns only companies the earlier runs did not deliver. A ready n8n workflow appends them to a Google Sheet every morning.

Price

$2.50 per 1,000 companies. A daily feed for one country is a few cents a week.

Sole traders appear in some registers under a person's name. Follow your local rules on direct marketing before you contact them.
