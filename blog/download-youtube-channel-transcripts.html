How to download the transcripts of a whole YouTube channel or playlist (without an API key)

2026-11-02 · 2 min read · ai rag content youtube

A channel's back catalogue is hours of expert talk that search engines and AI tools never see. Turning it into text makes it searchable, quotable and ready for an AI knowledge base, show notes or a newsletter. Copying captions one video at a time does not scale, and the official API does not hand out other people's captions.

This guide gets you the transcript of every video in a channel, playlist or search, as clean text with timestamps, for $2.50 per 1,000 videos.

What you get

One row per video:

FieldExample
Title, channel, linkthe video's details
Published, length, viewswhen, how long, how popular
Transcriptthe full text
Segmentseach caption line with its start time, for quoting or jumping to the moment
Caption language, auto-generatedwhich track was used, and whether it was typed by a person
Word count487 for a three-and-a-half-minute music video

Videos without captions are listed in the run summary and are not charged.

Step 1: open the tool

Go to YouTube Transcript Scraper and click Try for free. No Apify account? Create one free; no card needed. The free plan returns up to 100 transcripts per run.

Step 2: paste a channel, playlist or videos

json
{
  "videos": ["https://www.youtube.com/@YourChannel"],
  "maxVideosPerSource": 50,
  "languages": ["en"]
}

Use a playlist link, single video links or searchQueries the same way. Ask for another language with translateTo.

Step 3: run and export

Click Start. A single video comes back in about a second. Export as JSON for an AI pipeline, or as CSV or Excel for reading.

New videos only, every week

Add "onlyNew": true, "monitorName": "my-channel" and schedule it weekly: each run returns only the videos earlier runs did not deliver. A ready n8n workflow puts them in a Google Sheet every Friday.

Ideas

  • Feed a channel into a retrieval (RAG) knowledge base and answer questions with sources and timestamps.
  • Turn talks into blog posts, show notes or newsletter quotes.
  • Track what competitors and creators say about your product across their videos.
