Questions

Billing questions

Who bills me?

Apify. Prices, invoices and your spending limit live in your Apify account; each tool's Apify page is the source of truth if a number here differs.

Why do prices differ by plan?

Apify gives its paid plans a lower price per result. The figures here are the free-plan price, the highest you would pay.

Is anything charged besides results?

A tiny start fee per run (a fraction of a cent). A few tools also have optional extras, listed in their row: an AI insight on each item, every list entry checked, or each post read while looking for leads. Extras are charged only when you turn them on or they happen, and your spending cap covers them too. Nothing is charged for searches that come back empty.
