Privacy policy

What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do. Questions: tacps126@gmail.com.

Last updated 2026-11-13. Also: Terms of sale, Refunds, Data sources and removal.

Who is responsible

Tapaswai Choudhary, India, runs apirows.com and decides how the personal data described here is used (the controller under the GDPR, the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act).

Visiting the site

No analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The site remembers your light or dark theme in your own browser; that choice never leaves your device. Our hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and browser details to serve the pages and keeps them in its server logs under its own privacy policy.

Buying from us

When you buy, Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, collects your name, email, billing address and payment details. We receive your name, email, country, what you bought and the amounts; we never see your card details. We use them to deliver your files and refreshes, to answer you and to keep the records tax law requires. Legal basis: our contract with you and our legal obligations.

API accounts

For an API account we keep your email, your key (only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it back), your balance, and your runs with their inputs and results. Results are deleted after 7 days; runs and charges are kept as accounting records.

Writing to us

If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and as a record of what was agreed.

Companies in our data files

Our files describe companies, not private individuals: company name, booth, website, the company's business email and phone, postal address, product categories, the company's social media pages and its own description. Contact details are the ones a company publishes for business enquiries. Where such an address names a person (for example a sales contact listed in a show's exhibitor directory), it is included as published.

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interest, and our buyers' legitimate interest, in business-to-business contact about the products and events the company itself promotes. Where the data comes from is described on data sources and removal. You can object at any time and we remove the data. A show edition's files are deleted 12 months after the show; a removal list is kept so removed details do not come back.

Who else receives data

  • Dodo Payments: payments, tax and invoices.
  • Our hosting and email providers, to run the site and answer you.

We do not sell the personal data of visitors, buyers or API users and do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

In India and with providers in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Orders and invoices: as long as tax and accounting law requires (up to 8 years).
  • Emails: up to 3 years after the last message, longer only when they concern an order.
  • API results: 7 days.
  • Show files: 12 months after the show.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, object to its use, ask us to restrict it or to send it to you in a common format, and withdraw any consent you gave. Write to tacps126@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days, usually much sooner, and may ask you to confirm that the request comes from the person or company concerned. You can also complain to your data protection authority or, in India, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

The site and the files are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data about children.
